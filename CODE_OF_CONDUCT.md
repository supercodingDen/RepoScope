# Community conduct

RepoScope welcomes constructive, respectful technical discussion. Contributors and maintainers should focus on the work, assume good faith, provide reproducible evidence when reporting a problem, and avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive material, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation to keep the project usable. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private GitHub channel or another contact method the owner publishes. Do not include credentials, private source, or sensitive vulnerability details in public issues. The owner should acknowledge a report when a private contact channel is configured and handle it without unnecessary disclosure.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, documentation, and community spaces managed by RepoScope. It is a project-specific policy; no external code-of-conduct organization is implied.
